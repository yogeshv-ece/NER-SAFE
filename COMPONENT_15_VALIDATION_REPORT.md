### 1. Executive Summary & Scientific Baseline Protection

The existing, validated NER-SAFE system (Components C7 through C13) has been extended into a scientifically defensible **Pre-Landslide Temporal Forecasting and Early Warning System (C15)**.

In strict compliance with project directives:
- **Zero Work Overwritten**: C7 terrain derivatives, C8 Sentinel-2 indices (9.17 GB), C10 calibrated Random Forest susceptibility models, C11 flow paths and runout corridors (48 hotspots), and C12 CAP XML/JSON advisory feeds were completely preserved without alteration.
- **Clear Scientific Demarcation**: The system explicitly maintains mathematical and conceptual separation between:
  1. **Static Susceptibility**: Baseline terrain vulnerability derived from DEM and geomorphic slope units (C10 baseline).
  2. **Current Environmental Risk**: Operational four-factor heuristic fusion ($0.40 \cdot \text{Susceptibility} + 0.30 \cdot \text{Rainfall Anomaly} + 0.20 \cdot \text{Soil Moisture Anomaly} + 0.10 \cdot \text{S2 Surface Change}$).
  3. **Future Temporal Forecast**: Probability that a slope-failure initiation event will occur within a discrete future time window ($1\text{h}, 3\text{h}, 6\text{h}, 12\text{h}, 24\text{h}, 48\text{h}$).
  4. **Warning / Advisory**: Consequence-bounded advisory tiers generated by C12 subject to alert hysteresis and suppression safeguards.
  5. **Last Known Assessment**: Historical snapshot served when fresh data is unavailable.
  6. **Historical Replay Result**: Replayed historical episode (e.g., Cyclone Remal) explicitly labeled `REPLAY MODE`.

If qualifying new observations are missing or fail quality control, the system transitions to `WAITING_FOR_DATA`. It **never** reuses an old score as the current score, **never** converts missing data to zero, and **never** equates missing data with low risk.

### 4. Machine Learning Model Audit: Random Forest vs XGBoost

A rigorous comparison framework (`c15_model_comparator.py`) was implemented:

- **Why Random Forest for C10**: Tabular geomorphological data with nonlinear slope/lithology interactions, robust to unscaled terrain derivatives, resistant to overfitting on limited spatial samples, and interpretable via Gini importance.
- **Why Scikit-Learn**: Serves as the ML orchestration foundation—providing data preprocessing, time-series splitters, spatial-block K-fold, probability calibrators (`CalibratedClassifierCV`), and rare-event PR-AUC / Brier metrics.
- **Why XGBoost**: Scalable gradient-boosted decision trees capable of modeling asymmetric temporal decay, multi-window lag interactions, and weighted loss for severe class imbalance.
- **Validation Strategy**: Time-based holdout and spatial-temporal blocking (never random train/test splits, preventing temporal leakage).
- **Primary Metric**: **PR-AUC (Precision-Recall Area Under Curve)** and Brier score calibration, acknowledging that overall accuracy is misleading for rare disaster events.

### 6. Alert Fatigue & False-Alarm Safeguards

Disaster warning systems fail when excessive false positives breed public apathy. The `alert_safeguard_engine.py` enforces:
- **Dual-Threshold Hysteresis**:
  - Critical Tier: Triggers at $\ge 0.70$, clears only when dropping below $0.60$.
  - High Tier: Triggers at $\ge 0.52$, clears only when dropping below $0.44$.
- **Alert Suppression Window**: 4-hour cooldown prevents spamming identical alerts for the same hotspot.
- **Traceable Delivery States**: `GENERATED`, `QUEUED`, `SENT`, `DELIVERED`, `WAITING_FOR_NETWORK`, `FAILED`, `EXPIRED`. Alerts are never marked delivered until verified by network acknowledgement.

### 7. Degraded Network Hierarchy & Offline Preparedness

To address remote Northeastern village connectivity realities, `network_state_manager.py` implements a 4-level resilience protocol:

- **Level 1 (Online)**: Full live dashboard, bi-directional API, high-res tile streaming.
- **Level 2 (SMS / Cellular Fallback)**: Compact 160-character alert payloads delivered via cellular SMS gateway without requiring smartphone data.
- **Level 3 (Intermittent Connectivity)**: Outbox queuing with store-and-forward synchronization.
- **Level 4 (Complete Network Outage)**:
  - Local cached baseline risk maps and pre-computed runout envelopes.
  - UI strictly enforces:
    $$\text{CURRENT RISK: NOT AVAILABLE / AWAITING NETWORK}$$
    $$\text{LAST KNOWN ASSESSMENT: [Tier]} \quad (\text{Generated: [Timestamp]})$$
  - Locally stored citizen incident reports (`LOCAL_ONLY` $\to$ `SYNCED`).
  - Edge IoT gateway hooks for offline sensor siren triggers.

### 8. Citizen Reporting Abuse & Moderation Controls

To prevent malicious or false reports from polluting operational risk or poisoning future ML datasets:
- **Rate Limiting**: Maximum 5 reports per 10 minutes per authenticated user.
- **Proximity Deduplication**: Flagged if submitted within 50 meters and 1 hour of an existing report.
- **Evidence-Based Workflow**: States transition through `SUBMITTED` $\to$ `UNDER_REVIEW` $\to$ `VERIFIED` / `REJECTED`.
- **Model Isolation**: Citizen reports are **strictly isolated** from live C10/C15 risk engines. They can never trigger live alerts or retrain models without authorized administrative sign-off.
