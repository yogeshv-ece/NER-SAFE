How NER-SAFE Landslide Predictions Work

The NER-SAFE engine combines multi-source satellite remote sensing, digital elevation derivatives, calibrated machine learning, and empirical downhill runout routing into an operational decision-support pipeline.
Scientific & Operational Transparency Disclaimer:
Modelled runout corridors represent empirical gravitational likelihood corridors based on digital elevation gradients. The system uses defensible scientific terminology ("Predicted Potential Runout Corridor", "Estimated Potential Impact Zone") and does not claim to predict the exact second, micro-meter fracture plane, or guaranteed physical volume of slope failure.
